Frequently Asked Questions

To register at Tabora Boys Secondary School, follow these steps:
  • Download or collect the registration form from the school office or this portal.
  • Complete the form with accurate personal and academic details.
  • Attach all required documents (see the Documents section).
  • Submit the completed form at the Admissions Office or upload via the portal.
  • Pay the registration fee and retain your receipt.
Registration is open from January to March each year for the following academic year.
The registration deadline for the upcoming academic year is typically 31st March. Late applications may be considered subject to availability of places. We strongly recommend submitting early to secure your placement. Contact the Admissions Office at admissions@taboraboys.ac.tz for specific dates each year.
Applicants must meet the following criteria:
  • Have sat and passed the Primary School Leaving Examination (PSLE) or equivalent.
  • Be of Tanzanian nationality or hold a valid residence permit.
  • Meet the minimum grade requirement set by NECTA for admission to government secondary schools.
  • Be in the correct age range for Form One entry (typically 13–16 years).
Please bring originals and certified copies of the following:
  • PSLE/NECTA results slip (original + 2 copies)
  • Birth certificate or baptism card
  • National identification document (parent/guardian)
  • 2 recent passport-size photographs
  • Transfer letter (if transferring from another school)
  • Medical fitness certificate (from a registered medical officer)
  • Completed registration form
If you are missing documents due to extenuating circumstances, visit the Admissions Office in person with what you have. The admissions team can grant a conditional registration for up to 14 days while you gather the remaining documents. Missing documents after 14 days may result in cancellation of registration. Contact us at admissions@taboraboys.ac.tz to discuss your situation.
School fees are set by the government and are collected each term. The current structure is:
  • Registration fee: TSh 20,000 (once per year)
  • Tuition (per term): As per government guidelines
  • School uniform & books: Payable at registration
Payments are made at the school's designated bank account (details provided at the admissions office) or via the school portal. Always collect and retain your official receipt. For fee waivers or financial assistance, speak with the Head Teacher's office.
Fees are due on or before the first day of each term. Late payment may affect a student's ability to sit exams or receive reports. Parents are encouraged to pay at least one week before the term starts to avoid administrative delays. For approved payment plans, contact the Bursar's Office directly.
To reset your portal password:
  • Click "Forgot Password" on the portal login page.
  • Enter your registered email address or student ID.
  • Check your email for a password reset link (valid for 24 hours).
  • If you do not receive an email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.
If the problem persists, contact us at support@taboraboys.ac.tz with your student registration number.
If you experience technical issues with the portal, try the following:
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies, then reload.
  • Try a different browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended).
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
  • Disable any browser extensions that may interfere.
If the problem continues, submit a support ticket below and include a screenshot if possible. Our technical team will respond within 24 hours.
